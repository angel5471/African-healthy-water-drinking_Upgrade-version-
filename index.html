🌍 Lesson: Making Drinking Water Safe
(Mafunzo: Kufanya maji kuwa salama)

Water is necessary for life. But water can contain harmful germs (vijidudu) that make people sick.

Unsafe water may cause:

  • Diarrhoea (kuharisha)
  • Stomach pain (maumivu ya tumbo)
  • Water-related diseases

We can protect our health by following four safe water rules.

🔥

1. Boil Water Before Drinking
(Chemsha maji kabla ya kunywa)

The most effective and reliable method:

Boiling water is one of the safest ways to make drinking water better.

✅ What to do:

  • Put water into a clean pot.
  • Heat until the water boils strongly.
  • Wait until you see many bubbles.
  • Continue boiling for about 1 minute.
  • Allow the water to cool before drinking.

💡 Why does boiling work?

High temperature kills harmful germs and worm eggs that can cause diseases.

Boiling can be used for:

  • River water (maji ya mto)
  • Well water (maji ya kisima)
  • Tap water (maji ya bomba)
🔥 Remember:
Boiling = Using heat to kill germs
Chemsha = Kuua vijidudu kwa moto
🪣

2. Store Water Safely
(Hifadhi maji kwa usalama)

Clean water can become unsafe again if it is stored badly.

✅ What to do:

  • Use a clean bucket or container.
  • Always cover the container with a lid.
  • Use a clean long-handled spoon to collect water.
  • Never put your hands inside drinking water.

💡 Why is this important?

Flies, dust, dirty hands and objects can bring germs back into clean water.

A covered container protects water from becoming contaminated again.

🪣 Safe storage:
Clean container + Cover + Clean tool
Chombo safi + Funika + Kijiko safi
☀️

3. Use Sunlight When Boiling Is Difficult
(Tumia jua kusafisha maji)

Some families may not have enough firewood or electricity. In this situation, sunlight can help make water safer.

✅ Steps:

  1. If water is cloudy, filter it first using a clean cloth.
  2. Put the water into a clean transparent plastic bottle.
  3. Close the bottle tightly.
  4. Place it under strong sunlight for about 6 hours (from morning until afternoon).

💡 Why does sunlight work?

Sunlight contains ultraviolet rays (UV rays) and heat. They can kill many harmful germs in water.

This method is useful because:

  • It does not need firewood.
  • It does not need electricity.
  • It only needs sunlight.
☀️ Solar disinfection:
Clean bottle + Sunlight + Time
Chupa safi + Jua + Muda
🧼

4. Wash Hands Before Eating and After Toilet Use
(Nawa mikono kabla ya kula na baada ya choo)

Safe water alone is not enough. Dirty hands can bring germs into our mouths.

✅ What to do:

  • Wash hands with clean water and soap.
  • If soap is not available, use clean ash.
  • Rub all parts of your hands.
  • Wash for the time it takes to sing a short song.

💡 Why does hand washing matter?

Many germs enter our bodies through our hands. Clean hands help prevent disease.

🧼 Hand washing:
Clean water + Soap/Ash + Rubbing
Maji safi + Sabuni/Majivu + Kusugua mikono

⭐ The Four Safe Water Rules

🔥 1. Boil water before drinking.

🪣 2. Keep stored water covered.

☀️ 3. Use sunlight if boiling is not possible.

🧼 4. Wash hands before eating and after toilet use.

Clean water + Safe storage + Clean hands = Healthy people
